## About me

**Attention:** *I'm looking for a postdoc starting in summer 2027. If you know of any openings in programming/software engineering education, or just want to chat about collaboration, I'd love to hear from you!*

I'm Eduardo Carneiro Oliveira, a passionate educator and PhD candidate at Utrecht University, Netherlands. During my PhD, I conducted qualitative studies to investigate [student errors in code refactoring](https://doi.org/10.1145/3587102.3588840), and the [reasons behind these errors](https://doi.org/10.1145/3699538.3699550). Later, I studied [patterns of student interaction](https://doi.org/10.1145/3769994.3770000) with an LLM during refactoring tasks, and their relation to the quality of students' code. More recently, I designed [RPT+](https://doi.org/10.1145/3856208.3856231), a tutoring system with guardrails that provides LLM-generated feedback and hints on students' refactoring steps. The final experiment of my dissertation, currently underway, brings the previous studies together. It concerns students' use of RPT+ in the classroom, comparing two types of LLM-generated feedback and their relation to students' understanding of refactoring errors.
