## About me

I'm Eduardo Carneiro Oliveira, a passionate teacher and PhD candidate at Utrecht University, Netherlands. My research lies at the intersection of software engineering, computing education, and artificial intelligence, focusing on how pedagogical and technological approaches can support the learning and teaching of software engineering.
